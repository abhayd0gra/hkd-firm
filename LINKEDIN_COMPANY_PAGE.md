## ABOUT (Company Description)
*(max 2,000 characters — paste as-is)*

Harish Kumar Dogra & Co. is a full-service Chartered Accountancy firm practising from Jayanagar, Bangalore since 1982. Led by CA Harish Kumar Dogra, FCA — Fellow of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India — the firm has built over four decades of deep expertise in audit, taxation, GST, corporate law, and business advisory.

**What We Do**
We provide comprehensive financial and regulatory counsel to businesses across all stages of their lifecycle — from incorporation and structuring to statutory compliance, tax litigation, and M&A due diligence.

Our practice spans six core areas:
• Audit & Assurance (Statutory, Internal, Investigation)
• Direct Taxation (Returns, Assessments, ITAT Representation)
• GST & Indirect Taxes (Compliance, Advisory, Appellate)
• Corporate Law & ROC (Incorporation, Secretarial, Compliance)
• Business Advisory & Structuring
• Bookkeeping & MIS Reporting

**Who We Serve**
We work with businesses across manufacturing, hospitality, trading, real estate, e-commerce, agribusiness, and food processing — from established mid-size enterprises to growth-stage companies.

**Our Team**
Our practice comprises 3 qualified Chartered Accountants and 11 finance professionals, supported by a network of specialist legal counsels. We combine the personal attention of a relationship-driven firm with the depth of knowledge of a specialist practice.

**Our Commitment**
Every client — regardless of scale — receives the same rigour of analysis and quality of advice that the largest enterprises take for granted. That has been our founding commitment since 1982, and it remains our defining standard today.
